CAROL Privacy Policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

CAROL is a private, personal AI assistant. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, and is not offered to anyone else.

What Google data CAROL uses

  • Gmail (read-only): CAROL can search and read the owner's email when the owner asks a question about it. It cannot send, delete, or change email.
  • Google Calendar (events): CAROL can list the owner's events, add events when the owner asks, and delete an event only after the owner confirms.

How that data is handled

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Email and calendar content is read only to answer the owner's request. To produce an answer, the relevant text is sent to the AI service CAROL uses (OpenAI), under that service's API terms.
  • CAROL does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone else, and does not use it for advertising.
  • CAROL's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the developer contact email listed on CAROL's Google sign-in screen.
